## Optimization Types

| Type | Trigger | Impact | Risk |
|------|---------|--------|------|
| Create Index | High query frequency, low selectivity | 80-95% latency reduction | Low-Medium |
| Cache Warming | Hot key detection (hotness > 0.7) | 20-40% hit rate improvement | Very Low |
| Cache Resize | Hit rate < 70% or > 95% | 10-15% latency change | Low |
| Archive Data | Inactive > 90 days, large size | 30-70% storage reduction | Medium |
| Vacuum | Fragmentation > 30% | 15% I/O reduction | Medium |
